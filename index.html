About Us

Angeloma Drinks & Refreshments Limited is a leading FoodTech company specializing in cold chain logistics and beverage distribution. Based in Ogun State with operations extending across Lagos and beyond, we combine cutting-edge cold storage technology with reliable distribution networks to ensure beverages maintain optimal freshness from manufacturer to consumer.

Our expertise in cold chain management means every bottle, can, and package is transported and stored at the perfect temperature. We understand that in Nigeria's tropical climate, proper refrigeration isn't just about comfort—it's essential for quality and safety.

Whether you're a retailer, restaurant, event planner, or wholesaler, Angeloma Beverage Co delivers the refreshments your customers crave, exactly when and how you need them.

Our Services

Cold Chain Distribution

Temperature-controlled transportation ensuring your beverages arrive perfectly chilled. Our fleet of refrigerated vehicles covers Lagos, Ogun State, and surrounding regions with reliable daily deliveries.

Bulk Beverage Supply

Competitive wholesale pricing for retailers, supermarkets, restaurants, and event venues. We stock a comprehensive range of soft drinks, juices, bottled water, and energy drinks from trusted brands.

Cold Storage Solutions

Secure, hygienic storage facilities with 24/7 temperature monitoring. Perfect for businesses needing reliable cold storage for their beverage inventory.

Event Beverage Supply

Specialized service for weddings, corporate events, parties, and festivals. We deliver, set up cooling stations, and handle all your refreshment needs so you can focus on your guests.

Retail Partnership Program

Flexible supply agreements with competitive margins for small and medium retailers. We support your business growth with reliable stock availability and excellent service.
